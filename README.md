# Bunker Blues

A Fallout Shelter Adventure

By Nate Taylor

With thanks to David Etherton for helping encouraging me to get this to completion.

You are a junior crew member, who has grown up in a nuclear fallout shelter after an apocalyptic war. The shelter having run out of supplies, a couple of others have gone to investigate a safe place they heard about on the radio. You were left with an older crew member, but an earthquake caused a fire and took systems offline, leaving you just enough time to get to the airlock.

Even if it were safe to go outside, you didn’t have time to get your survival helmet, and the outer doors wouldn’t open anyway without being disengaged, which requires electricity. So to escape you will need to find a way to see, to breath, and to restart the generators.
Your only companion will be the sometimes humorous, sometimes annoying cleanbot.
